UGANDA

Uganda is a landlocked country in East Africa known for its rich natural beauty and diverse wildlife. It lies along the equator and is often called the “Pearl of Africa” because of its scenic landscapes, which include lakes, mountains, and savannahs. The country is home to Lake Victoria, the largest lake in Africa, and the source of the Nile River.
